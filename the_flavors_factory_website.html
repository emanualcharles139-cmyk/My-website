Located conveniently at the T-Point of KG Marg in Connaught Place (at the landmark Old Amber Restaurant building), The Flavors Factory (थे फ्लेवर्स फैक्ट्री) brings together authentic South Indian recipes, golden crispy dosas, slow-brewed brass filter coffee, and hearty North Indian favourites under one roof.

Dosa Mastery

From classic golden Mysuru dosas to modern melted cheese garlic fusion dosas.

Generous Portions

Fresh slow-simmered sambar, tri-color chutneys, and rich curries.

Warm Ambience

Peaceful family environment, quick courteous service, and air-conditioned dining.

Reasonable Pricing

Premium food quality in Connaught Place at reasonable pricing (₹200–₹1,000/person).
